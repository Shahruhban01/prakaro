# Phase 9 executive reporting

Organization administrators see their organization's persisted completions, pending backlog, workforce, QA, AUX, adherence and estimated capacity. Platform organization comparison is restricted to Super Admin. Managers select from their authorized teams, LOBs and queues. Live presence, work in progress and current staffing are snapshot metrics. Historical measures use explicit local date bounds.

AHT is weighted by accepted completions. QA accuracy is finalized passes divided by finalized evaluations and includes its sample size. Open disputes remain outside finalized QA. Sampling coverage uses automatic selected samples over Phase 7's eligible population, excluding manual selection from that ratio. Scope without a defensible denominator shows Unavailable.

The Phase 6 team report continues to use today's roster. Phase 9 team outcomes count work inside a recorded assignment for that team, with matching work-time organization and LOB. Team AUX clips intervals to those assignments. Work without a team snapshot remains unattributed to a historical team. No team membership is inferred for legacy work. Current team roster is presented separately.

Backlog uses the existing current pending-case definition and deduplicates the same case across queues for organization totals. A team's share of a common backlog is unavailable. Executive capacity describes tomorrow in the reporting timezone and uses configured staffing rather than assuming that logged-in staff will be available.
